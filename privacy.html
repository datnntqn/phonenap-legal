Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version: PhoneNap runs on your iPhone. It has no ads and no account. Which apps you use and for how long never leave your phone. The only thing sent is optional, anonymous usage stats (which screens you open, what you buy), and you can turn them off in the app.

What PhoneNap stores

Everything PhoneNap keeps is stored on your iPhone only:

  • Your pet: its name, kind, outfits and mood.
  • Your progress: food, gems, points, streak, streak shields and focus session history.
  • Your settings: daily focus goal, daily summary time, notification and Live Activity choices.
  • Your app groups and rules: limits, wait times, open limits and schedules.

This data lives in PhoneNap's private storage and in an App Group shared only with PhoneNap's own widgets and Screen Time extensions, so they can show your pet and apply your rules. It is not synced to any server. If you use iCloud or encrypted device backups, Apple may include it in your backup under Apple's privacy policy.

Screen Time data

PhoneNap uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings) to pause the apps you choose and to show how long you used them.

  • When you pick apps, Apple gives PhoneNap opaque tokens, not a list of the apps on your phone.
  • Usage time is measured by Apple on your device. PhoneNap's report screen runs inside a sandbox Apple provides, which has no network access.
  • App names and minutes are never sent anywhere, by PhoneNap or by its extensions.

You can turn off PhoneNap's Screen Time access at any time in iOS Settings, under Screen Time.

Anonymous usage stats

From version 1.3, PhoneNap can send anonymous usage stats to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU, to learn which features help and where people get stuck.

  • What is sent: events such as "opened Stats", "finished a focus session", "saw the Pro screen" or "bought Pro", with simple details like the pet's level, the streak, whether Pro is active, the plan chosen and how many apps a group holds. Purchases include the price, currency and country from the App Store.
  • What is never sent: which apps or websites you use or pick, how many minutes you spend, your pet's name, or anything that tells who you are.
  • No identifier that points to you: TelemetryDeck receives a one-way hash, so it can count users without knowing them. No IP address is stored, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking across apps.
  • Your choice: Settings › Account & privacy › Share anonymous usage stats. It is on for new installs and off for anyone who installed PhoneNap before version 1.3, as promised here earlier.

What PhoneNap does not do

  • No advertising, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No account, no sign-in, no email or name collected inside the app.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • The only third-party SDK is TelemetryDeck, for the optional stats above. No data is sold or shared for advertising.

Because nothing is tracked, PhoneNap never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Notifications, widgets and Live Activity

Notifications such as the nightly summary are scheduled by iOS on your device. PhoneNap does not register for push notifications. Widgets and the focus Live Activity are drawn from the data on your phone and update on the device.

Purchases

PhoneNap Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; PhoneNap learns whether Pro is active and, with usage stats on, sends the plan, price and country of a purchase anonymously. Your payment details are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send PhoneNap's developer anonymous crash reports. You control this in Settings, under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

In PhoneNap, open Settings, then Account & privacy, then Delete all data. This removes your pet, food, points and streak from your iPhone. Export my data, on the same screen, gives you a copy first. Deleting the app also deletes all of its data.

Children

PhoneNap collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Emailing support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply, and we delete the conversation when it is resolved, unless you ask us to keep it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of PhoneNap changes what is described here, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change. Any new kind of data collection will be explained here, and in the app, before it starts.
